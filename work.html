After your booking is confirmed, the saree is sent to the Paramesh Saree Prepleating service location. Please pack the saree safely when sending it to us and keep your courier details until the service is completed.

📦 Pack your saree safely before sending

Once the saree is received, we process it according to the prepleating style selected during booking. The saree is handled carefully throughout the preparation process.

✦ Selected style is followed

After prepleating, your saree is checked and prepared for return. We pack it properly so the prepleated saree can be transported safely.

📦 Checked • Folded • Packed

The professionally packed saree is sent back to the address provided in your booking. The return courier charge is handled separately and confirmed according to the applicable courier cost.
